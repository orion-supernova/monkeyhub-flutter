🍌 Feature design · games economy

Bananas & Unlocks

Every game starts free with a taste of everything. The rest is unlocked with bananas, once, forever, for that person. In a chat, the friends you invite play your game for free, but to send a game themselves they need their own unlock. Kids can't buy; they ask, and a parent pays from their own bananas.

1

The four rules

Everything below follows from these. If a screen or a server check contradicts one, the screen is wrong.

1
🌱

Free to start, never a wall

Every mode is playable on day one: 5 fighters, a stage, the Cartoon look, 4 chat games, every daily. Locked things are visible, previewable and priced, never hidden.

2
🔑

Unlock once, yours forever

An unlock belongs to the account, not the phone. It follows you to Mac, web, a new phone. No rentals, no expiry, no subscriptions.

3
🎟️

Your key, your invites

To send a game you must own it. Everyone you invite plays that match free. To send one back, they need their own unlock.

4
👪

Kids ask, parents pay

Child accounts never see a price in money. They tap Ask; a guardian approves and pays in their own bananas, and the unlock lands on the child's account.

2

What exists today

The banana is already the currency (built 2026-11-16, design/rewarded-ads.md). It only buys lifelines: small rescues in the moment you are stuck. There is no in-app purchase and nothing is locked.

💰 Earn

Rewarded video: 1 🍌, every 3rd video of the day 2 🍌. Cap 15 videos a UTC day (≈ 20 🍌). Free daily banana for every account, children included. iOS and Android only for videos.

🛟 Spend (lifelines)

Extra Code guess 1, extra hint 1, bot takeback 1, Jungle Fist get-back-up 1, streak save 2. These stay exactly as they are.

🧱 Pieces we reuse

lifelines wallet row (one per user), BananaPill + showBananaWallet sheet, AdMob SSV crediting, the guardian queue (family:listGuardianQueue) and its push, BananaWallet.childAccount.

💡

The gap: bananas have nothing to save up for. Lifelines are spent the moment they're earned. Unlocks give bananas a goal, give videos a reason past "I'm stuck", and give paying players a way to skip the wait.

Your key, your invites

The host is whoever sends the game into the chat (the match row's creator). Sending needs the host to own it. The host's unlock sponsors that match, so everyone invited plays free. It does not carry over: a guest who wants to send the game back needs their own unlock.

The rules, exactly

SituationWhat happens
Start a gameAllowed if the game is free or the host owns it. The server checks at matches:create; the picker only mirrors it.
Join / acceptAlways allowed, for every seat (2 players or a party of 8). Joining never checks the joiner's unlocks.
Send a game backA guest who doesn't own it can't send it. The picker shows the lock and the unlock sheet. Being invited never grants the right to send.
RematchCounts as sending. The host can rematch any time. A guest who owns the game can rematch too; a guest who doesn't gets Ask Murat for a rematch (a ping; Murat's tap starts it on his unlock) next to Unlock · 🍌 150.
Jungle Fist stageThe host picks from the host's stages (or Random among them).
Jungle Fist fightersEach player picks only from their own fighters. A guest with no unlocks plays the 5 free humans. Unlocks are never shared, even when both players own the game.
Jungle Fist lookEach screen shows its owner's look (Cartoon if they own nothing else).
Host loses the unlock mid-match (refund clawback)The running match finishes. New sends and rematches check again.
Host is a childSame rule. A child who was given Chess can host Chess for friends.
Bot games & TrainingSolo, so you are the host: your own unlocks (plus free Training trials, see section 3).
Public roomsSame rule. Hosting a game you own in a public room lets everyone in it play.
🎟️

Why this works for growth: one paying friend can invite anyone, so every guest gets to play the game first. When they want to send it back, that's the moment to unlock, after they already know they like it. The locked game is the invitation, not a paywall.

Motion

🍌 Unlock

A real moment: the lock shakes, pops open, a short banana burst from the button and the item lifts into place with a jelly spring and a success haptic. Under a second.

Request rules

🧺 Limits

At most 3 open requests per child, one per item. A "Not now" can be re-asked after 24 hours. Open requests close themselves after 7 days.

👥 Two guardians

Every guardian gets it; the first answer wins (approvals are never quorum, same as contact requests). The other guardian's card turns into "Dad said yes".

🔁 Already sorted

If the child saves up and unlocks it with their own bananas, or the other guardian already said yes, the open request closes itself as "done".

🛡️ Safety

Children never reach a store, a pack, or a money price. The note is filtered like any message. Guardians pay from their own wallet, so an approved request can never spend the child's bananas.

🎓 Turning 13

When a family account graduates, every unlock and every banana stays. It's the same account.

🍎 Apple & Google

The guardian buys packs on their own device with normal IAP (and Ask to Buy if they use it there). The child app has no purchase code path at all.

Edge cases & platform rules

The questions support will get, answered up front.

CaseAnswer
Web, Linux, WindowsUnlocks and bananas work everywhere (it's the account's wallet). Packs are sold through RevenueCat on iOS, macOS and Android. RevenueCat has no Windows SDK; until that's decided, Windows, web and Linux show "Get bananas in the app", like videos today.
Apple cross-platform ruleContent bought on one platform can be used on others, as long as it's also buyable through IAP on iOS (guideline 3.1.3(b)). Every pack is.
RefundRevenueCat's REFUND / CANCELLATION webhook takes the pack's bananas back. The balance may go below zero; spending stops until it's positive. Unlocks are never removed.
Restore purchasesNot needed: packs are consumables credited to the account server-side, and unlocks live on the account. A "Missing bananas?" row re-checks unfinished transactions.
Account deletedWallet, unlocks, ledger and requests are cascaded with the account (referenceRules.ts). Bought bananas are not refundable by us; the delete sheet says how many will be lost.
Old app buildsThe server enforces the host rule on matches:create, so an old build can't start a locked game; it gets a "Update to play" error. Ships with a minimum-version bump (R19).
Existing players on launch dayNothing they've played is taken away silently: every account gets a 🍌 150 thank-you plus the free first pick, and in-progress matches of now-locked games finish normally.
Gifting to friendsNot in v1. Only guardians can pay for someone else. Friend gifting is a later idea (section 12).
LifelinesUnchanged. They draw from the same wallet.

Backend & cost

Follows backend/backend-handbook.html: no per-user jobs, no fan-out, zero cost for people who never touch it. Ownership rides on the wallet row everyone already has.

🗄️ Data

  • lifelines (existing, one row per user) gains owned: string[] (catalog ids, ≤ 40), firstPickUsed, firstPackUsed, allowance fields. Ownership costs 0 extra reads anywhere the wallet is already read.
  • bananaLedger (new, append-only): one row per purchase, unlock, gift, allowance, refund. by_user, by_storeTx (dedupe). Support and refunds read it; nothing subscribes to it.
  • bananaRequests (new): child, item, price, note, status, decidedBy. by_child_status. Expiry is checked on read, no cron.
  • matches (existing) gains sponsorId (= creator, set only when they own the game; rematches re-check like any new send).
  • Catalog: backend/convex/catalog.ts is the truth (ids, prices, bundles, free set), mirrored by lib/games/catalog.dart like the game registries. Prices come back in lifelines:mine, so a price change needs no app release.

⚙️ Functions

  • lifelines:mine also returns owned and the price list.
  • unlocks:buy {item}: price check, complete-the-set math, one patch + one ledger row.
  • RevenueCat: purchases_flutter in the app, app user id = the Convex user id. RevenueCat validates receipts; its webhook (http /revenuecat, bearer secret) calls internal.store:credit, deduped on the store transaction id. Same shape as AdMob SSV: the app never credits itself.
  • Refunds arrive on the same webhook.
  • matches:create: +1 check against the host's owned.
  • bananaRequests:ask / answer / withdraw; family:listGuardianQueue folds open requests in.
  • family:setBananaRules, family:sendBananas.

12

Rollout

Four phases, each shippable on its own. Backend first, then the app, as always.

Phase 1

Catalog & locks

Catalog, owned, unlock sheet, locked states in Jungle Fist and the picker, host rule, Training trials, first free pick, launch-day 🍌 150. Bananas only (ads + daily). Minimum-version bump.

Phase 2

Banana packs

5 consumables in App Store Connect and Play Console, wired into RevenueCat (offering bananas). RevenueCat webhook, ledger, shop sheet, first pack doubled. Privacy labels: purchases.

Phase 3

Kids ask

Ask sheet, requests table, guardian queue cards, push both ways, Send bananas, allowance, auto-yes, history.

Phase 4

Polish & later

Complete-the-set pricing, bundles, puzzle archive, seasonal stage drops, gifting to friends (needs abuse limits), new fighters as their own unlocks.

13

Decisions

Answered 2026-11-25. Each is still a single line to change.

1

Which chat games are free? Decided

Tic-tac-toe, Connect Four, Dots, Jungle Fist (Imposter moved to paid, 2026-11-25). Chess is locked; it's the one most people expect free, so it's the first candidate to swap in if conversion is fine without it.

2

Free look Decided

Cartoon is the free default look (user, 2026-11-25).

3

No sharing Decided

The host's chat game unlock lets guests play that match. Fighters, looks and packs are never shared: a guest uses their own, or the free defaults (user, 2026-11-25).

4

Prices and pack sizes Decided

80 🍌 per $0.99 base, fighter 60, Chess 150, Complete 990. Tuned so an animal fighter is 3 max-video days. Worth one round of tester feedback before Phase 2.

5

Launch-day gift size Decided

🍌 150 + first free pick for every existing account, instead of grandfathering whatever they played. Simpler, and it teaches the shop on day one.

6

Payments via RevenueCat Decided

RevenueCat for every store (user, 2026-11-25). Open: Windows, which RevenueCat has no SDK for.
