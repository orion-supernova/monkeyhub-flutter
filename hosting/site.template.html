MonkeyHub

Rooms, friends, secrets that fade.

A chat app for the people you actually talk to. Start a room, call someone, send a voice note — or open a secret room, where every message deletes itself on a timer you set.

Open the web app Download

The web app runs in your browser — nothing to install.

💬

Rooms, not group chats

Public rooms anyone can find, private ones behind a password. Replies thread properly, reactions land instantly, and you can see who has read what.

🤫

Secret rooms

Pick a lifetime when you create the room and every message in it deletes itself when the clock runs out. No cleanup, no history to comb through.

📞

Calls that ring

Voice and video, peer-to-peer. On a phone an incoming call takes over the lock screen like a real one, so you don't miss it in a notification pile.

📸

More than text

Photos from the built-in camera or your gallery, video, and voice notes — recorded and played back without leaving the conversation.

👨‍👩‍👧

Built for family

Pair a child's device with a code. Guardians approve who they can talk to, set the rules, and can hand the device back whenever they like.

🎨

Make it yours

Themes, chat styles, reply styles and room layouts — MonkeyHub does not have to look like everyone else's MonkeyHub.

Everywhere you are. Use it right now in a browser at the web app, side-load the Android APK, or get it on iPhone through TestFlight and the App Store. Everything syncs as it happens.
One thing the browser can't do: ring you when the tab is closed the way a phone can — so for calls, the app is the better seat.
