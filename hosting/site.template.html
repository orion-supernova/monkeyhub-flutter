Parental controls

How do I set up a supervised account?

On your own account, open your profile → Family → Add a child. Pick their name and username, then tap Create + pair. You become their guardian.

How do I pair their device?

Install MonkeyHub on their device and tap Set up a child account on the sign-in screen. Then scan the code on your phone, or type its six digits. Need a fresh code later? Tap Pair under their card in Family. Each code works once and expires quickly.

How do I approve contacts?

They can talk to their guardians and siblings straight away. Everyone else needs your OK first, both people they ask for and people who ask for them. Requests wait for you in Family, and you can remove a contact at any time.

What can guardians see?

When they're online and who they talk to, not their messages. Guardians control who gets through and which features are on (calls, photos and video, new contacts, creating rooms), rather than reading conversations.

Can a second parent help?

Yes. In the child's settings, add another grown-up by username. They get exactly what you have: every request to approve and every setting to change.

Do supervised accounts see ads?

No. GIF & sticker search shows them no sponsored tiles at all, and uses KLIPY's strictest content filter.
